Zero-Interruption Background Sync

You never have to leave Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube. ELION runs silently in the background on your laptop and transcribes videos automatically.

1. Native Instagram "Save" Sync BACKGROUND
Just tap the normal Bookmark / Save icon inside Instagram on your phone. The laptop daemon polls your Saved folder in the background, downloads audio-only, and runs Whisper locally.
2. In-App Mobile Share Webhook INSTANT
Point an iOS Shortcut or Android "HTTP Share" target to this background webhook. Tapping Share → Save to ELION inside Instagram/TikTok queues the video in 10ms without ever leaving your app:
http://localhost:8080/api/webhook/save?url=
3. Clipboard Auto-Capture
Tap "Copy Link" inside Instagram or TikTok (works across phone & laptop via Windows Phone Link / Universal Clipboard). ELION detects the link in the background and transcribes it automatically.
